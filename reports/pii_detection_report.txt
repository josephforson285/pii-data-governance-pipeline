7. RE-IDENTIFICATION RISK
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Masking direct identifiers does not make a dataset anonymous. Grouping
rows by quasi-identifiers (birth year, postal code, income band) shows
how many people each record is hidden among.

Quasi-identifiers modelled: date_of_birth, address_postal, income
Signatures with an unparseable component: 7.7% - these share a common
'unknown' signature, which groups them together and overstates
their protection.

GROUP SIZE          ROWS      PCT
k=1 (unique)        5000   100.0%

100.0% of records are unique on quasi-identifiers alone (k=1).
Those individuals stay re-identifiable after every direct identifier
is masked, by anyone holding a second dataset with the same attributes.

The masked output is therefore pseudonymous, not anonymous, and remains
personal data under GDPR Recital 26. Genuine anonymisation would need
generalisation of the quasi-identifiers to reach a k threshold.
