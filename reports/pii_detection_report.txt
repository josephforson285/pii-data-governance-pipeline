100.0% of records are unique on quasi-identifiers alone (k=1).
Those records are uniquely distinguishable on the modelled
quasi-identifiers, so they carry elevated linkage risk wherever
matching auxiliary data exists. Masking direct identifiers does
not change that.

The masked output should be treated as pseudonymised rather than
anonymous for this assessment: k-anonymity alone does not settle
that question, and Recital 26 keeps pseudonymised data in scope.
Reaching a k threshold would need further generalisation.
