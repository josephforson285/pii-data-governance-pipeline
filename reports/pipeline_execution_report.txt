2. STAGE ORDER
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Post-clean validation gates publication: the cleaned extract is
written only after it satisfies the schema it claims to satisfy.

Masking is likewise verified before release: the masked frame is
re-scanned for direct identifiers, and nothing reaches disk unless
the scan is clean.

PII detection runs on the raw file, before cleaning: exposure is a
property of what landed on disk, and scanning post-clean would
understate it by every quarantined row.

Validation runs twice. A single post-clean pass only proves the clean
data is clean; the pre/post delta is what shows remediation worked.
